    Business automation

    The jobs nobody wants to do on a Friday afternoon

    Every small business has a handful of tasks that eat hours and add nothing. They get done late, or twice, or not at all. We find those tasks, wire them into the tools you already pay for — Xero, Gmail, Microsoft 365, your booking system — and let them run on their own.

    What we usually start with

    • Overdue invoicesReminders go out on day 7, 14 and 21 without you having the awkward conversation.
    • Receipts and billsEmailed invoices are read, coded and filed into your accounting system automatically.
    • Enquiries and quotesWebsite enquiries land in one list, get an instant reply, and text whoever is on call.
    • Bookings and remindersCustomers book themselves in, get a confirmation, and a reminder the day before.
    • Timesheets and payroll prepHours are collected, totalled and sat ready for approval every Monday morning.
    • Stock and reorderingWhen a line drops below its minimum, the purchase order is drafted and waiting.
    • ReportingOne email every Monday with the numbers you actually look at. No dashboard to log into.

    You keep the controls

    Automation people distrust is automation people switch off. So everything we build shows its working: a plain-English log of what ran, what it changed, and what it skipped. Anything involving money or a customer can be set to ask you first.

    • Runs inside your own accounts — you own it, not us
    • Every action logged and reversible
    • Approval step on anything that spends or sends
    • If it breaks at 2am, we get the alert, not you

    Questions

    Before you ring

    We're only four people. Is automation worth it for us?

    Usually more than for a big company, because there's nobody spare to absorb the admin. The businesses that get the most out of this are the ones where the owner is still doing the invoicing at night. If the audit shows there's nothing worth automating, we'll say so.

    Do we have to change the software we use?

    Almost never. We build around Xero, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Shopify, Tradify and most common booking systems. Changing tools is expensive and unpopular, so we treat it as a last resort.

    What happens if an automation goes wrong?

    Every action is logged and reversible, and anything that sends or spends can be set to need your approval first. Failures alert us, not you, and we fix them under your care plan.

    Is our data going overseas?

    It stays in your own accounts, under your own logins. We work inside your systems rather than copying your data into ours. If a tool we recommend stores data offshore, we'll tell you before you sign up to it.

    How quickly do you respond to a security alert?

    Within 15 minutes during business hours on Watch & Respond and Fully Managed. Serious alerts — ransomware behaviour, a confirmed account takeover — are actioned after hours too.

    Do you still fix laptops and phones?

    Yes. Repairs, data recovery, custom desktop builds and sales all carry on exactly as they have since 2002. Drop in to 634 Pollen Street any weekday.
