Questions

Before you ask for a quote

How long does a new site actually take?

A starter site is usually ready in one to two weeks. A business site with a blog and booking system typically takes two to three weeks. Online stores take three to five weeks depending on how many products need loading.

Who owns the site once it's built?

You do — outright. The domain, hosting account and every file are registered in your name, and we hand over every login at launch. There's no platform lock-in and nothing you have to keep paying us to keep using.

Is SEO included, or is that extra?

Every site launches with the basics done properly — page titles, site speed, mobile-friendliness and a Google Business Profile. The ongoing Local SEO care plan is separate, for businesses that want someone actively working their rankings every month rather than a one-off setup.

What if I already have a domain or some content?

Even better — that comes off the price. The fixed quote on the scope call accounts for what you already have, so you're never paying to redo work that's already done.
