About us

Twenty-odd years of other people's computers

Locally owned in Thames since 2002 — straight answers, no lock-in contracts, and a proper workshop on Pollen Street.

Computer Geeks started in 2002, working out the back of the Thames laundromat. Our geeks drove around the district fixing machines on kitchen tables and shop counters. These days there's a proper store and workshop on Pollen Street, and a lot of what used to need a drive gets fixed remotely.

What hasn't changed is who we work for: the businesses and families around here. We're 100% locally owned, we build our own computers from parts we'd put in our own machines, and when you ring, you get someone in Thames who knows your setup.

Automation is the newest part of what we do, and it comes from the same place — we kept watching good local businesses lose whole days to admin that a computer should have been doing for them.
